How We Onboard a Client

From payment to live. The snapshot does the build, the settings do the personalising. Same flow for every client and trade.

Start (payment)
Automatic (GHL)
Guided setup (you)
1

Client signs up and pays Start

The client buys the plan for their trade on a SaaS order form and starts a Stripe subscription. Payment triggers everything.

Automatic on payment
2

Account created, snapshot attached Automatic

GHL provisions the sub-account and applies the snapshot for their trade (electrician, plumbing or survey/quote): pipeline, workflows, bot, calendars.

3

Wallet and rebilling activate Automatic

The client's card is attached to their usage wallet. Phone, WhatsApp and AI usage bill at your markup, with auto-recharge.

Guided setup · a few hours
4

Capture their details Guided

Collect the settings: name, area, hours, team (callback owner + backup), fees, radius, website, email. Mostly the same for everyone; a few vary by trade.

5

Buy their number Guided

One new HighLevel number, registered for WhatsApp. This is their calls and WhatsApp line.

6

Fill in the settings Guided

One step personalises the whole system. Their details update the bot script, messages, fees and callback target everywhere. No workflow editing.

7

Set team and calendars Guided

Add the callback owner and backup, and assign them to the calendars.

8

Confirm the copy Guided

Quick check the customer messages read right for them, in the client's language.

9

Test end to end Guided

Fire a test enquiry: missed call → acknowledgement → bot → callback task → outcome → downstream. Confirm it all fires.

10

Go live and train Guided

Publish, hand over the one-page phone guide, and walk them through the three outcomes (call, visit, job).

11

Monitor the first leads Guided

Watch the first real enquiries and tweak if needed.

The snapshot builds it. You just fill in the blanks. Same every time.

Once the snapshots are solid, a new client is stood up in a few hours, not weeks.
