How a Job Flows

From first enquiry to review. One human decision, automation carries the rest.

Customer
Automatic (the system)
Human (Petterson / Alef)
The key decision
1

Enquiry comes in Customer

A customer calls the number (missed or after hours), or messages on WhatsApp or web chat.

2

Instant acknowledgement Automatic

A WhatsApp reply goes out at once so they know they've been seen. A callback task is created and the 15-minute clock starts.

3

Assistant gathers the basics Automatic

In Portuguese, it asks only: area, the problem, a safety signal, and best callback time. Danger flag → straight to a human, no long chat.

4

Human calls back Human

Petterson (or Alef as backup) calls within 15 minutes, with the assistant's summary to hand. Not done in time → escalates to backup.

5

One decision Key moment

After the call, the tradesperson taps one outcome on their phone:

Urgent visit Standard visit Survey / quote Request photos Future follow-up Refer / out of scope Resolved No answer / reschedule
6

System does the rest Automatic

That one tap moves the stage, creates the next task, sends the right message, and opens the right calendar.

7

Visit or survey Human + reminders

If booked, reminders go out (WhatsApp + email) to cut no-shows. Missed → no-show recovery kicks in.

8

Quote, if the job needs one Automatic chase

Quote prepared and sent. The system chases it on a timer until there's a decision, and stops the moment the customer replies.

9

Decision Human

Won → work scheduled. Lost → closed with a reason recorded. No dead deals left hanging.

10

Job done → reputation Automatic

Marked completed → triggers a review request. Optional future maintenance or reactivation later.

One human decision. Automation carries it the rest of the way.

Running underneath: every job has an owner and a next action, overdue callbacks and quotes surface, and response time and conversion are tracked.
